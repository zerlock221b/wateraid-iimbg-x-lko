Technical Challenges

Engineering and infrastructure obstacles encountered in the field

These are the key WASH infrastructure and sustainability challenges identified across both the Bayer (AWC-level) and Viega (Project-level) sites during the 8-week internship. Each card presents the challenge, its nature, and current status.

🏗️ Bayer Project — AWC-Level WASH Gaps
🔧

Infrastructure Shortages

Most AWCs rely on old handpumps as their primary water source. Installation of solar submersible motors revealed leaking and rusted pipes, causing technical delays. Lack of boundary walls exposes water purifiers and solar panels to risk of damage.

Ongoing
🚻

Non-Functional Sanitation

At select AWCs, toilet blocks have a non-functional design with no water supply, rendering them unusable. This directly limits the promotion and practice of hygiene among beneficiaries.

Pending
🏦

Community O&M Sustainability

Though 7 Boond Banks are functioning in Lucknow with 22 Jal Sakhis supporting AWCs, the long-term sustainability of O&M groups requires continued follow-up and structured capacity strengthening.

Ongoing
🧠

Hygiene Behaviour Change Effectiveness

WASH Warriors conduct hand hygiene and safe water handling trainings using a Training of Trainers (ToT) model reaching 160 AWC representatives. However, the measurable impact of these behavioural interventions has not yet been empirically validated.

Under Review
🌊 Viega Project — Project-Level WASH Problems
🏗️

FSTP Technical Weaknesses

The 9-bed Faecal Sludge Treatment Plant (FSTP) shows poor output water quality due to insufficient filtration of septic tank effluent through buffer holes. This structural weakness is currently being rectified.

Being Rectified
🏛️

Community Governance Gaps

ULB employees managing O&M show capacity gaps affecting sustainability. Water Management Committees (WMCs) need strengthening, and the helpline 1533 objection remedy mechanism is yet to be fully developed.

Pending
♀️

Gender Inclusion

WMCs currently have 60% female and 40% male representation, which is promising. However, increasing women in leadership roles must remain a programme priority — this target still requires field validation.

In Progress
♻️

Maturity of Waste Management

The Material Recovery Facility (MRF) with a Conveyer-Segregator-Hydraulic Press system is in place. Its measurable impact on waste diversion behaviour and community participation requires separate evaluation.

Under Evaluation
