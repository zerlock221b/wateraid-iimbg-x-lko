Viega Project

Pond Rejuvenation in BKT — Restoring ecosystems and water security

Project Overview

The Viega Project focuses on ecological and hydrological restoration of degraded village ponds in Bakshi Ka Talab (BKT), Lucknow. The intervention addresses water scarcity, groundwater depletion, and poor wastewater management in peri-urban areas.

The project integrates pond rejuvenation with circular wastewater systems, linking it to long-term water security. It is aligned with climate resilience and sustainable WASH governance models.

Pond Rejuvenation

Desilting, bund repair, inlet-outlet restoration

Wastewater Integration

Linking ponds with treated water from FSTP

Ecological Restoration

Native plantation to improve biodiversity

Community Involvement

Engagement through Water Management Committees (WMCs)

Project Scale

58+ degraded ponds across BKT peri-urban clusters

Primary Method

Dredging, Bund Strengthening & Nature-Based Solutions

Impact Goals

  • Ensure year-round water availability through restored pond storage capacity and hydrological channel repair.
  • Support groundwater recharge by increasing percolation from functional water bodies.
  • Enable agricultural water security for farming communities dependent on local water sources.
  • Create a circular water economy model integrating treated wastewater reuse with pond systems.
  • Improve climate resilience in vulnerable peri-urban regions through sustainable water infrastructure.

Expected Outcomes

  • Restoration of functional water bodies across BKT
  • Activation of circular water reuse systems
  • Reduction in waterlogging and pollution
  • Strengthened community-led water governance
  • Scalable model for small-town WASH ecosystems
