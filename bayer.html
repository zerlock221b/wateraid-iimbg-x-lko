Bayer Project

Improving WASH infrastructure at Anganwadi Centres across Lucknow

Project Overview

The Bayer Project focuses on rehabilitating Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) infrastructure at Anganwadi Centres — government-run early childhood development centres serving children under 6 and pregnant/lactating mothers. Key interventions include toilet block rehabilitation, handwashing station installation, and boundary wall construction.

Centres Covered

14+ Anganwadis

Focus Area

Sanitation & Hygiene Education

Primary Challenges

  • Installed infrastructure is largely non-functional, especially water filters (8/13 AWCs), creating false assurance instead of safe water access.
  • Operations & maintenance systems are weak, with breakdowns remaining unresolved and community repair mechanisms underperforming.
  • Sanitation facilities are either absent or unusable, often due to lack of water linkage, leading to unsafe practices like open defecation.
  • Hygiene behaviour change has not been effectively internalised, with training failing to translate into consistent community practices.
  • Institutional support and accountability are insufficient, risking long-term sustainability once external intervention reduces.
