Challenges Faced

Bayer (Anganwadi): Technical delays are caused by rusted or leaking pipes in legacy handpumps. A critical safety risk exists due to the absence of boundary walls, exposing new infrastructure to damage.

Viega (BKT): Poor septic tank designs (75% failure rate) lead to direct wastewater discharge into open drains. Behavioral issues, such as solid waste dumping, persist despite community mobilization. Additionally, outdated 2011 Census data restricts access to modern urban funding like AMRUT.
